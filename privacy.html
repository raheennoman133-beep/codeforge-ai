Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2024

Introduction

At Astra AI, your privacy is our top priority. We are committed to protecting your personal information and ensuring you have full control over your data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our AI chat services.

What We Collect

Chat Messages

  • Your chat messages are stored locally in your browser
  • Never sent to external servers
  • Only used to maintain conversation context during your session

Usage Data

  • Anonymous usage statistics (message count, session duration)
  • Browser type and device information
  • Theme preferences and chat history metadata
✅ No personal identifiable information is collected
✅ No chat data is stored on our servers
All data stays in your browser via localStorage

How We Use Your Data

  • Provide and improve our AI chat functionality
  • Store your chat history locally for your convenience
  • Track free tier message limits (resets daily)
  • Save your theme and UI preferences
  • Never sell or share your data with third parties

Data Control & Deletion

  • Clear All Chats - Delete all conversation history
  • Delete Individual Chats - Remove specific conversations
  • Logout - Clears all local data
  • Browser Storage - Clear site data in browser settings
All your data is stored only in your browser's localStorage.
When you clear browsing data or logout, everything is permanently deleted.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser localStorage (not cookies) to:

  • Save your chat history
  • Remember your theme preference
  • Track free message usage
  • Store Pro subscription status

You can clear this data anytime through your browser settings.

Third Party Services

This website uses only trusted third-party services:

  • Google Fonts - Fonts (no tracking)
  • Font Awesome - Icons (CDN)
  • No analytics or tracking services

Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be announced in the chat interface.
